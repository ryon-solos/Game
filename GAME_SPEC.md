# Horror Street Game: Spec

## Overview
A horror game where a monster roams a long street lined with tall apartment blocks. Players hide inside the flats, listen for the monster, and survive 10 nights. Playable solo or multiplayer.

## Setting
- A long, continuous street.
- Buildings on both sides, each 3 to 4 floors high.
- Players can enter the flats and hide inside.

## Goal
- Collect stars on the street.
- Use stars to get onto the next night.
- Survive all 10 nights.

## Monster
- Roams the street at night.
- Has 8 hands: 4 on the right side, 4 on the left.
- Reaches its hands into flats to find players.
- If a hand grabs a player, that player is out for the night (game over for them).
- Hears sounds from inside the flats. Noise can attract it.

## Night Progression
- Night 1 to 10: the monster gets faster, scarier, more powerful, and smarter each night.
- The monster's height increases each night.
- Nights 1 and 2 (open questions, see below): on the terrace and 4th floor, the monster's low height causes it to randomly destroy flats with its hands.

## Multiplayer
- Solo mode: one player.
- Multiplayer mode: multiple players, each hiding in different flats.
- Sound from one player's flat can draw the monster toward others (to be confirmed).

## Core Mechanic: Sound
- Sound is the main tension driver.
- Quiet hiding is safer; noise raises risk.

## Daily Hook (idea)
- Each day the street layout can change, so players can't memorise the route.
