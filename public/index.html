    How to survive

    • Collect stars. They lie in the street, on balconies, on terraces and inside flats. Gather the night's quota and dawn comes.
    • It hears you. Sprinting, doors, landing from a jump and gunshots all carry. Crouch-walking is silent.
    • Its hands come through the windows. One touch and you are out for the night. Back rooms behind a closed door are safer, on the early nights.
    • Do not stay put. Thirty seconds in one place and it simply knows. Watch the presence meter in the bottom left.
    • It grows. Each night it is taller, faster, has more hands in use, reaches deeper, remembers longer. From night five it opens doors. From night six it pretends to leave.
    • On nights one and two it is too short to reach the top floors and terraces, so it smashes them instead. Debris hurts.
    • Flare pistols stun it for a few seconds. The shot tells it exactly where you are.
    • Be indoors at dawn to rest and recover health. Falls hurt. Bandages help.
