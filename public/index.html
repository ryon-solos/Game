    How to survive

    • Take the stars. They lie in the street, on balconies, on terraces, in flats and shut inside closets. Face one and press E. It takes a moment, and the star cries out: the thing learns where it was taken. Grab it and move.
    • It hears you. Sprinting, doors, landing from a jump and gunshots all carry. Crouch-walking is silent. When it hears or sees you it comes fast.
    • Its hands come in through the windows and reach every room. A closed door only slows a hand down. One touch and it lifts you to its mouth.
    • Closets. Open one with E, step in, press E again to pull it shut. A hand that is only feeling around passes over a closed closet. A hand that knows you are there opens it.
    • Do not stay put. Thirty seconds in one place, closet or not, and it simply knows. Watch the presence meter in the bottom left.
    • Crawlers. From night three it calls things into the buildings: when you have half the stars, when it is angry, and from time to time. They hunt the stairwells and flats for about a minute, bite, and shriek your position to the street. They sometimes pull closets open.
    • It grows. Each night it is taller, faster, uses more hands and remembers longer. On nights one and two it cannot reach the top floors and terraces, so it smashes them. Debris hurts.
    • Flare pistols stun it for a few seconds. It wakes up furious and knows where the shot came from.
    • Be indoors at dawn to rest and recover health. Falls hurt. Bandages help.
